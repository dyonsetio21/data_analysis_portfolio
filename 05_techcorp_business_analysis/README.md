# TechCorp Business Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the business performance of **TechCorp**, an e-commerce company specializing in electronic products. Using SQL (MySQL), the analysis explores customer purchasing behavior, product performance, sales revenue, and customer support operations to generate actionable business insights.

---

## Business Problem

To improve business performance, companies need to understand customer purchasing patterns, product demand, sales performance, and customer service efficiency. By analyzing operational data, businesses can identify opportunities to increase revenue, strengthen customer relationships, and improve support quality.

This project answers several business questions, including:

- Who are the company's most valuable customers?
- What is the average customer spending?
- Which products are underperforming?
- How productive is the customer support team?
- What is the company's total revenue?

---

## Project Objectives

- Identify top customers based on purchase value
- Analyze customer spending patterns
- Evaluate employee performance in handling support tickets
- Identify products with low demand
- Calculate overall business revenue

---

## Dataset

The analysis uses a relational database consisting of six tables:

- `Products`
- `Customers`
- `Orders`
- `OrderDetails`
- `Employees`
- `SupportTickets`

---

## Tools

- SQL (MySQL)

## 2. Average Order Value

---

# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Develop loyalty programs for high-value customers to increase retention.
- Use Average Order Value to design personalized promotions and cross-selling strategies.
- Evaluate underperforming products through pricing reviews or targeted marketing campaigns.
- Recognize high-performing customer support agents while identifying opportunities for team development.
- Monitor revenue regularly to support business planning and performance evaluation.
