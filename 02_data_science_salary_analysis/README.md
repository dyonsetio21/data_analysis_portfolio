# Data Science Salary Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes salary trends across data science-related roles using SQL in Google BigQuery. The analysis explores how factors such as experience level, job title, employment type, and work location influence employee compensation.

The goal is to uncover salary patterns that can help organizations develop competitive compensation strategies while providing career insights for aspiring data professionals.

---

## Business Problem

Compensation is one of the most important factors in attracting and retaining data talent. Companies need to understand market salary trends, while professionals need reliable insights to make informed career decisions.

This project addresses several business questions, including:

- How does experience level affect salary?
- Which data science roles receive the highest compensation?
- How does employment type influence salary?
- Does work location impact salary levels?
- How have salaries changed over time?

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyze salary distribution by experience level
- Compare salaries across job titles
- Evaluate salary differences by employment type
- Analyze the impact of work location on compensation
- Identify salary growth trends over time

---

## Dataset

Public dataset containing salary information for various data science roles, including:

- Experience Level
- Employment Type
- Job Title
- Salary
- Company Location
- Employee Residence
- Remote Ratio
- Work Year

---

## Tools

- SQL (Google BigQuery)

# Key Findings

## 1. Impact of Experience Level on Salary

![result1](https://github.com/dyonsetio21/data_analysis_portfolio/blob/main/02_data_science_salary_analysis/results/salary_based%20_on_experience_level.png)

### Insights

- Average salary consistently increases with experience level.
- Executive-level positions receive the highest compensation.
- Entry-level professionals earn significantly less than senior roles.

---

## 2. Salary by Job Title

![result2](https://github.com/dyonsetio21/data_analysis_portfolio/blob/main/02_data_science_salary_analysis/results/salary_based_on_job_position.png)

### Insights

- Data Analytics Lead and Principal Data Engineer recorded the highest average salaries.
- Machine Learning Scientist showed a wide salary distribution, indicating differences in specialization and experience.
- Leadership and specialized technical roles generally command higher compensation.

---

## 3. Employment Type and Salary

![result3](https://github.com/dyonsetio21/data_analysis_portfolio/blob/main/02_data_science_salary_analysis/results/salary_based_on_employment_type.png)

### Insights

- Contract (CT) positions offered the highest average salaries.
- Full-time (FT) employees received competitive compensation along with employment stability.
- Freelance (FL) and Part-time (PT) roles tended to have lower average salaries.

---

## 4. Work Location Analysis

![result4](https://github.com/dyonsetio21/data_analysis_portfolio/blob/main/02_data_science_salary_analysis/results/salary_based_on_work_location_and_company_location.png)

### Insights

- The United States and Canada offered the highest average salaries.
- Employees working remotely for companies in high-paying countries often benefited from more competitive compensation.
- Salary levels generally reflected the company's geographic location.

---

## 5. Salary Growth Trends

![result5](https://github.com/dyonsetio21/data_analysis_portfolio/blob/main/02_data_science_salary_analysis/results/the_highest_salary_increase_from_middle_level_to_senior_level.png)

### Insights

- Salary growth from Mid-level to Senior-level positions was substantial.
- The largest increase was observed in 2020.
- Salary progression varied across years, suggesting the influence of market conditions and industry demand.

---

# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Organizations should benchmark salaries against market standards to remain competitive.
- Career progression has a strong impact on earning potential, highlighting the value of continuous skill development.
- Companies hiring internationally should consider regional salary differences when designing compensation packages.
- Professionals seeking higher salaries may benefit from pursuing senior-level or specialized technical roles.
