# Rossmann Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

Rossmann is one of the largest drugstore retail chains in Europe. This project analyzes sales performance across Rossmann retail stores using Microsoft Excel for data exploration and Microsoft Power BI for interactive dashboard development. The analysis focuses on identifying the factors that influence sales performance, including promotions, store types, weekdays, seasonal patterns, and school holidays. 

The goal is to provide business insights that support data-driven decisions for improving sales performance and optimizing retail operations.

---

## Business Problem

Retail businesses rely on sales data to evaluate promotional effectiveness, understand customer purchasing behavior, and optimize store performance. By analyzing historical sales data, management can identify key factors that drive revenue and improve operational planning.

This project answers several business questions, including:

- Do promotions significantly increase sales?
- Which store type generates the highest sales?
- Which days produce the strongest sales performance?
- Are there seasonal sales patterns?
- Do school holidays influence customer purchases?

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyze the impact of promotions on sales
- Compare sales performance across store types
- Identify weekday sales patterns
- Explore seasonal sales trends
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Generate business recommendations for retail operations

# Key Findings

## 1. Promotion Effectiveness

### Insights

- Sales increased by approximately **38%** during promotional periods.
- Promotions had a significant positive impact on store revenue.

---

## 2. Store Type Performance

### Insights

- **Store Type B** achieved the highest average daily sales.
- The result suggests advantages in store location, customer demographics, or operational strategy.

---

## 3. Weekly Sales Pattern

### Insights

- Sunday and Monday recorded the highest sales.
- Saturday generated the lowest average sales, indicating an opportunity for targeted promotional activities.

---

## 4. School Holiday Impact

### Insights

- Sales increased by approximately **4.4%** during school holiday periods.
- Although the increase was relatively modest, school holidays contributed positively to overall sales performance.

---

## 5. Seasonal Sales Trends

### Insights

- Sales consistently peaked in **December**, likely driven by holiday shopping and year-end promotions.
- Seasonal demand should be considered when planning inventory and marketing activities.

---

# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Schedule promotional campaigns during lower-performing days, particularly Saturdays.
- Study the operational strategies of Store Type B and apply successful practices to other stores.
- Increase staffing and inventory during high-demand periods such as Sundays and December.
- Take advantage of school holiday periods through targeted marketing campaigns.
- Use historical sales trends to improve sales forecasting and inventory planning.
